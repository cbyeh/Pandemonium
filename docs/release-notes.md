A dark-fantasy souls-like boss rush for the N64, inspired by Elden Ring.

- **Five bosses to learn:** the Guardian and Guardian Unbound, then three harder Bonus bosses: the Fallen Warrior, the Shackled Sun and Shadowborne, a crystal dragon with sweeping beams and fire. Every attack has a tell and hits where it's drawn.
- **The Tower:** a roguelite climb of floors and circles. Pick Boons, risk Pacts, survive Nights, carry a Relic, and spend Embers on permanent upgrades.
- **Combat with weight:** hit-stop, perfect dodges, Elden Ring-style stance breaks, and three skills to learn: Moon Blade Dance, Halfmoon Slash and Crescent Cleave.
- **Built for the N64:** 60 fps fights on hardware, eight skins (three earned), three save slots and three button layouts.
