# Test aids reference

All scripts live in `hle/autoplay.c` and need `-DHLE_AUTOPLAY` (HLE: `HLE_EXTRA_CFLAGS`; hardware/Ares: `TEST_CFLAGS`, the only way to drive Ares). Check a flag still exists with `git grep -n FLAG src hle` before relying on it.

## Put the game in a state
- **Straight to a boss:** `HLE_TEST_DUMMY HLE_DUMMY_BOSS=1..5` (Guardian, Unbound, Fallen Warrior, Sun, Shadowborne). Taps through menus, skips cutscenes; player is invincible and logs `[dummy]` damage over 120 s plus openings.
- **Second half / phase 2:** `HLE_DUMMY_HALF` (boss starts at 45% HP). `HLE_DRAGON_DARK` starts Shadowborne dark.
- **Full fights that never die:** `HLE_TEST_BONUS` (+ `HLE_TEST_BONUS_SUN` / `_DRAGON`), `HLE_TEST_UNBOUND`, `HLE_TEST_STRIKE`, `HLE_TEST_CRUCIFIX`: refill the player below 60 HP, log `[dmg]`.
- **One attack on a loop:** `HLE_FK_FORCE=1..9` (6 Triangle Slam, 7 Holy Slam, 8 Plunge, 9 Moon Blade), `HLE_SHOW_HURTBOX` also draws the bosses' strikes cyan while they can hit (the Fallen Warrior's blade in each swing window, the Sun's falling body, Shadowborne's cone forefeet; `[strikevol] f<frame>`), and `HLE_STRIKES_OFF` turns off the 2026-11-06 touches for before shots; `HLE_TOUCH_UNDER` (with `HLE_DRAGON_FORCE=21`): the player set where Shadowborne's first forefoot comes down in the fire cone (its touch: `[touch] cone forefeet`; the Sun's dive body `[touch] sun dive body`, the Fallen Warrior's blade grazes `[graze] st <move>`, all with `HLE_TEST_DUMMY`), `HLE_HOLY_ROLLIN` (with `HLE_FK_FORCE=7`): the dummy rolls toward the middle 0.1 s before its band of the Holy Slam's fire flares, then stands a second (`HLE_HOLY_ROLL_Y=-70` rolls away); `HLE_FK_HITLOG` logs each burst that reaches it, `[spread] dodged|BURNT: burst <n> (<band>) ...` (bursts from the middle: 0 comes with the slam itself, nothing to roll in to), `HLE_SUN_FORCE=1..7` (`11` = lash into sweep), `HLE_DRAGON_FORCE=<DgState>`, `HLE_SUN_HITME` (a sword hit every 1.2 s, every third a finisher: the Sun's reactions), Unbound's `HLE_THROW_FORCE` and `HLE_SWEEP_FORCE` (the ground sweep at any range: the dummy stays close). `HLE_DRAGON_CYCLE`: every Shadowborne move in turn (a gallery, `[cycle]` lines).
- **Long stagger:** `HLE_STAGGER_LONG` gives Crushing Blows outside the Tower; `[stagger]` lines log when each stagger starts, ends and the boss is up.
- **Into phase 2:** `HLE_TEST_TWO_RUNS` (kill the Guardian, reach phase 2, twice); `HLE_2R_NOSKIP` lets the cutscenes play (about 3000 frames), `HLE_2R_LOCK` locks on before the kill, `HLE_2R_KILL_T=<frames>` fights that long first (topped up, moving, swinging: a real fight records far more blocks).
- **Display lists:** `HLE_TEST_DL_CHECK` walks every task as the plugin will (into each `G_DL`) and logs any command this backend never emits (`[dlcheck] BAD`: Delta's plugin aborts on one), cache resets and replays while one is pending.
- **Boss held still** (before/after shots that line up): `HLE_TEST_FREEZE_BOSS`. Boss AI is otherwise non-deterministic. `HLE_DRAGON_FREEZE_CONE` stops Shadowborne's clock once a fire cone is out (with `HLE_DRAGON_FORCE=21`): a still scene to price what it draws.
- **Ending:** `HLE_TEST_ENDING`.
- **Lock-on at the kill:** `HLE_DUMMY_LOCK_KILL` (with `HLE_TEST_DUMMY`): locked on and circling, the boss dies at 4 s; `[lockkill]` logs the camera.
- **Effects alive:** `HLE_LOG_FXN` logs `[fxn] <live> live, player state <n>` once a second (with `HLE_DUMMY_REAL` the player dies: state 7 is the death screen).
- **Bot playtest:** `HLE_DUMMY_REAL` + `HLE_DUMMY_STRIKE` / `_ROLL`, `HLE_STRIKE_OPEN`, `HLE_STRIKE_STAMINA`; potions below 40%, prints `[bot] ... beaten/died`.
- **Player:** `HLE_DUMMY_ORBIT` / `KEEP` / `PIN` / `CENTER` / `MOVE` / `SIDESTEP`, `HLE_DUMMY_SKIN=<n>`, `HLE_PAD_LAYOUT`, `HLE_SKILLS_OFF`.
- **Skills:** off on a fresh save (pressing buttons casts nothing). `HLE_TEST_SKILL_DMG=<1-based>` zeroes cooldowns and casts on a loop (`[sdmg]` damage a cast); `HLE_SDMG_TOWER` does it in a Tower run knowing every skill (Lv 1). Stage 5: `HLE_SKILL_LV=<skill 1-3>,<lv 1-5>,<form 1 A, 2 B>` (with `HLE_SDMG_TOWER`) sets that skill's level and form; `lv` 0 steps each cast through Lv 1-4, 5 A, 5 B (cast 6 on is the second round: the first casts run while the Tower run starts). `[sdmg]` lines name the level and form and the stagger meter's peak; `HLE_SDMG_CYCLE=<frames>` a cast's cycle (200; Full Moon Dance needs 300), `HLE_SDMG_DIST=<units>` the player's distance from the boss (30.0f), `HLE_SDMG_FT` the frame time through each cast (`[sdmgft]` mean, worst), `[sdmgcyc]` each cast's start (ms and the VI frame, about: dense shots from it), `HLE_SDMG_STAGGERED` the boss held down with Executioner, 3 Breakers and Eclipse (`[stagbuild]`: the bonuses capped at x2). `HLE_LOG_SKILLHIT`: each cross, blade and arc that hits, where it was drawn against the boss's body (`[skillhit]`: a cross's or blade's middle from the capsule's surface against its drawn half-width, an arc's front against the body's edge and its angle). `HLE_DUMMY_CAST=<skill>` (with `HLE_TEST_DUMMY`): that skill cast every `HLE_DUMMY_CAST_EVERY` frames (300) in a Tower run begun at the fight, locked on, its cooldown zeroed; with `HLE_SKILL_LV` the casts alternate Lv 1 and that level; `=4` casts Moon Blade Dance, Full Moon Dance, Halfmoon Slash, Crescent Cleave, Crescent Storm in turn (`[palcast]`), and `HLE_PALCAM=<80.0f>` sets the player 60 from the boss each cast under a still camera that far behind him (effects against the boss); `HLE_SKILL_PALETTE=1` draws the player's skill effects in Sunforged (the floor-100 reward, preview; 0 today's cyan); `[castft]` each cast's frame time (mean, worst) and the most effects alive (busiest: `HLE_DUMMY_BOSS=4 HLE_SUN_FORCE=6`, the Sun's spiral). A spawn that finds the effect pool full logs `[fxfull]`. `HLE_TEST_TOWER` also runs `[skills]` (stage 5: the levels and forms, the forms' draft, Temper and the Pool, the B forms at the Shrine, the stagger cap, the pick math). With `=1` (Moon Blade Dance, first cast only): `HLE_MBD_KD=<frames>` a 25 hit that many frames in (knocked down mid-air); `HLE_MBD_CLOSECAM=<0.13f>` a still side camera that fraction of the follow camera's distance (`HLE_MBD_CAMYAW`, 1.2 rad); `HLE_MBD_XSTILL=<28.0f>` no cast, one X held still in the air, seen from behind that far (the camera's turn barely shows).
- **Tower:** `HLE_TEST_TOWER_RUN`, `HLE_RUN_MASH` (dodge every 15 frames from each kill: `[mash]` logs how long each draft stayed up and the card taken), `HLE_RUN_CLIMB`, `HLE_RUN_STRIKE` (`HLE_RUN_TOPUP`: never dies), `HLE_TOWER_SB_LAST`, `HLE_RUN_FLOORS`, `HLE_RUN_PICK`, `HLE_TOWER_GIVE`, `HLE_TOWER_RELIC`, `HLE_PACT_CURSE`, `HLE_DRAFT_GALLERY`, `HLE_DRAFT_HOLD`. `HLE_TEST_TOWER`: the rules' self-check, now with the slots, levels, a full bar, the packs, family weighting and the unlock bits (`[slots]`). `HLE_TOWER_PACKS=<bits>`: climb with these families' packs (bit per family: Blood 1, Storm 2, Moon 4, Iron 8, Shadow 16, Hearth 32: Prospector, stage 4). Stage 2 (rivals, "+", duos): `HLE_TEST_TOWER` also runs its self-check (`[stage2]`: a duo only with two maxed boons of different families and both packs, the merge opening a slot, rivals never both offered or held, "+" only at the max, each "+"/duo effect by the numbers, the Build rows). `HLE_TOWER_GIVE=BOON_<NAME>` takes a "+" with its boon maxed, or a duo with a maxed boon of each family merged into it and every skill; with `HLE_BOONFX_RUN` (and `HLE_TEST_TOWER_RUN HLE_RUN_STRIKE HLE_LOG_BOONFX HLE_RUN_FLOORS=2 HLE_STRIKE_FRAMES=3000`) each duo's effect fires in the fight (the run also casts a Cleave at t 300 and lands a blow 1 s after its potion). `HLE_GALLERY_FROM=<page>` starts `HLE_DRAFT_GALLERY` on that page (2: the boons from the first; 15-22: the "+" and duo cards; a duo card with no pair shows each family's longest name). `HLE_TOWER_GIVE_NEW`: every pack boon at its top level (past the 5 slots) and every skill. `HLE_LOG_BOONFX`: each new boon's effect as it fires (`[boonfx]`); `HLE_BOONFX_RUN` (with `HLE_TEST_TOWER_RUN HLE_RUN_STRIKE HLE_TOWER_GIVE_NEW HLE_RUN_FLOORS=2 HLE_STRIKE_FRAMES=3000`) sets up each trigger in a Guardian fight: a potion, rolls with a blow inside them (perfect dodges), a blow after, a big blow, a Cleave on each stagger. `HLE_STRIKE_FRAMES`: `HLE_RUN_STRIKE`'s fight length (1200). `HLE_TEST_PACKS`: Shrine > Unlocks buys Blood, Storm and Shattering Dance (the first B form, 200) on a save with none (a second boot of the same ROM buys nothing); `HLE_LOG_TOWERSAVE` (also in it) logs the slot's Tower progress and `_pad[24..27]` at boot (`[tsave]`: old saves, a reboot). `HLE_TEST_TOWER_STATS`: a full bar (two Blood, two at their max, a new boon) and every skill and pact, through the pause menu's Stats and Build pages and Controls by A alone (`[stats]` steps); `HLE_STATS_SEQ="\"...\""` walks the pause menu its own way (S, D/L/R, A, B; e.g. `SDABAABDDDABB`: B closing Stats from each page and Controls), `HLE_TITLE_SEQ` presses on the title's menus instead of climbing (`DDDDADDADDAABB`: Settings > Controls, its Back, B back out); `HLE_STATS_BARE` holds nothing (e.g. with `HLE_TOWER_RELIC=112`, the Sage). Stage 3 (the map): `HLE_TEST_TOWER` also runs its self-check (`[map]`: 1,000 seeded maps checked path by path against section 2's rules, the generator's checker turning each rule down, 80 circles walked by the screens' own presses, the circle scaling by maps, a Rest's heal/potions/Temper/Release by its screens, an Elite's draft, health and Embers). `HLE_MAP_WALK` (with `HLE_TEST_TOWER_RUN`): a path that enters a Rest in rows 2-5 and an Elite when it can, moving with the D-pad and stick in turn, entering with A and Up in turn; `HLE_REST_SEQ="\"T1Hk\""` the Rests' choices (H/T/P, then k keep or n release the n-th row); `[walk]` lines (with `HLE_LOG_MOVE_MS`, the VI frame). `HLE_TOWER_SEED=<n>`: the run's seed (the map, drafts; seed 5's first circle has a Rest then an Elite). - No timescale, fast-forward or RNG seed aid exists (boss order seeds from the clock). Speed up by skipping to the state above, not by running faster.

## Capture
