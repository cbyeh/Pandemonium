# Test aids reference

All scripts live in `hle/autoplay.c` and need `-DHLE_AUTOPLAY` (HLE: `HLE_EXTRA_CFLAGS`; hardware/Ares: `TEST_CFLAGS`, the only way to drive Ares). Check a flag still exists with `git grep -n FLAG src hle` before relying on it.

## Put the game in a state
- **Straight to a boss:** `HLE_TEST_DUMMY HLE_DUMMY_BOSS=1..5` (Guardian, Unbound, Fallen Warrior, Sun, Shadowborne). Taps through menus, skips cutscenes; player is invincible and logs `[dummy]` damage over 120 s plus openings.
- **Second half / phase 2:** `HLE_DUMMY_HALF` (boss starts at 45% HP). `HLE_DRAGON_DARK` starts Shadowborne dark.
- **Full fights that never die:** `HLE_TEST_BONUS` (+ `HLE_TEST_BONUS_SUN` / `_DRAGON`), `HLE_TEST_UNBOUND`, `HLE_TEST_STRIKE`, `HLE_TEST_CRUCIFIX`: refill the player below 60 HP, log `[dmg]`.
- **One attack on a loop:** `HLE_FK_FORCE=1..9` (6 Triangle Slam, 7 Holy Slam, 8 Plunge, 9 Moon Blade), `HLE_SUN_FORCE=1..7` (`11` = lash into sweep), `HLE_DRAGON_FORCE=<DgState>`, `HLE_SUN_HITME` (a sword hit every 1.2 s, every third a finisher: the Sun's reactions), Unbound's `HLE_THROW_FORCE` and `HLE_SWEEP_FORCE` (the ground sweep at any range: the dummy stays close). `HLE_DRAGON_CYCLE`: every Shadowborne move in turn (a gallery, `[cycle]` lines).
- **Long stagger:** `HLE_STAGGER_LONG` gives Crushing Blows outside the Tower; `[stagger]` lines log when each stagger starts, ends and the boss is up.
- **Into phase 2:** `HLE_TEST_TWO_RUNS` (kill the Guardian, reach phase 2, twice); `HLE_2R_NOSKIP` lets the cutscenes play (about 3000 frames), `HLE_2R_LOCK` locks on before the kill, `HLE_2R_KILL_T=<frames>` fights that long first (topped up, moving, swinging: a real fight records far more blocks).
- **Display lists:** `HLE_TEST_DL_CHECK` walks every task as the plugin will (into each `G_DL`) and logs any command this backend never emits (`[dlcheck] BAD`: Delta's plugin aborts on one), cache resets and replays while one is pending.
- **Boss held still** (before/after shots that line up): `HLE_TEST_FREEZE_BOSS`. Boss AI is otherwise non-deterministic. `HLE_DRAGON_FREEZE_CONE` stops Shadowborne's clock once a fire cone is out (with `HLE_DRAGON_FORCE=21`): a still scene to price what it draws.
- **Ending:** `HLE_TEST_ENDING`.
- **Lock-on at the kill:** `HLE_DUMMY_LOCK_KILL` (with `HLE_TEST_DUMMY`): locked on and circling, the boss dies at 4 s; `[lockkill]` logs the camera.
- **Effects alive:** `HLE_LOG_FXN` logs `[fxn] <live> live, player state <n>` once a second (with `HLE_DUMMY_REAL` the player dies: state 7 is the death screen).
- **Bot playtest:** `HLE_DUMMY_REAL` + `HLE_DUMMY_STRIKE` / `_ROLL`, `HLE_STRIKE_OPEN`, `HLE_STRIKE_STAMINA`; potions below 40%, prints `[bot] ... beaten/died`.
- **Player:** `HLE_DUMMY_ORBIT` / `KEEP` / `PIN` / `CENTER` / `MOVE` / `SIDESTEP`, `HLE_DUMMY_SKIN=<n>`, `HLE_PAD_LAYOUT`, `HLE_SKILLS_OFF`.
- **Skills:** off on a fresh save (pressing buttons casts nothing). `HLE_TEST_SKILL_DMG=<1-based>` zeroes cooldowns and casts on a loop (`[sdmg]` damage a cast); `HLE_SDMG_TOWER` does it in a Tower run holding every skill and the Halfmoon+/Cleave+ cards. With `=1` (Moon Blade Dance, first cast only): `HLE_MBD_KD=<frames>` a 25 hit that many frames in (knocked down mid-air); `HLE_MBD_CLOSECAM=<0.13f>` a still side camera that fraction of the follow camera's distance (`HLE_MBD_CAMYAW`, 1.2 rad); `HLE_MBD_XSTILL=<28.0f>` no cast, one X held still in the air, seen from behind that far (the camera's turn barely shows).
- **Tower:** `HLE_TEST_TOWER_RUN`, `HLE_RUN_MASH` (dodge every 15 frames from each kill: `[mash]` logs how long each draft stayed up and the card taken), `HLE_RUN_CLIMB`, `HLE_RUN_STRIKE` (`HLE_RUN_TOPUP`: never dies), `HLE_TOWER_SB_LAST`, `HLE_RUN_FLOORS`, `HLE_RUN_PICK`, `HLE_TOWER_GIVE`, `HLE_TOWER_RELIC`, `HLE_PACT_CURSE`, `HLE_DRAFT_GALLERY`, `HLE_DRAFT_HOLD`. `HLE_TEST_TOWER_STATS`: a climb holding every boon at its most and every pact twice, through the pause menu's Stats pages and Controls by A alone (`[stats]` steps); `HLE_STATS_SEQ="\"...\""` walks the pause menu its own way (S, D/L/R, A, B; e.g. `SDABAABDDDABB`: B closing Stats from each page and Controls), `HLE_TITLE_SEQ` presses on the title's menus instead of climbing (`DDDDADDADDAABB`: Settings > Controls, its Back, B back out); `HLE_STATS_BARE` holds nothing (e.g. with `HLE_TOWER_RELIC=112`, the Sage). `HLE_TOWER_HEALCHECK` (with it): the healing boons measured in the fight (`[heal]`: a skill's hit heals 0, a sword hit 12% a stack of its base, Rally the whole blow, 4 a sword hit, Phantom Step 2%). `HLE_LOG_FWTINT`: the Fallen Warrior's armour colour as drawn (each load should read 100,27,34).
- **Saves:** `HLE_TEST_SAVE_FLOW`, `SAVE_FRESH`, `SKIN_SAVE`, `SKIN_SLOT` dump EEPROM block 0; `HLE_LOG_RELIC` after a reboot. Isolate a run's saves with `M64P_EXTRA="--set Core[SaveSRAMPath]=/tmp/x"`; back up `Pandemonium-72AF2497.eep` before editing one.
- No timescale, fast-forward or RNG seed aid exists (boss order seeds from the clock). Speed up by skipping to the state above, not by running faster.

## Capture
- **Cameras:** `HLE_DUMMY_SIDECAM` (`HLE_SIDECAM_K`), `HLE_DUMMY_CAM` (`HLE_DUMMY_CAM_YAW` turns it round the boss, 1.57 = side; `HLE_DUMMY_CAM_DIST`, 120), `CAM_NEAR`, `CAM_HIGH`, `CAM_PORTRAIT` (`HLE_PORTRAIT_D/H/LOOK/TURN`, `HLE_PORTRAIT_FLIPX` for the Shackled Sun, whose front is the other way; with `HLE_SUN_FORCE=9` it only hovers, its face at rest). `HLE_SKIN_GATE` poses skins at the fog gate. `HLE_SKIN_CYCLE` steps through the caped skins (at the gate, 300 frames each; with `HLE_BOOT_SKIN`, on the title); `HLE_LOG_HEM` logs the cloak's hem above the ankle against knee, hip and pins, in the units of a skin's cloth length.
- **Hit capsules drawn:** `HLE_SHOW_HURTBOX` (with `HLE_TEST_FREEZE_BOSS`, `HLE_LOG_MOVE_MS`; `HLE_NO_HUD` for clean shots): the player's capsule green, the boss's magenta, over every fight frame; its script poses the knight then the Black Swordsman, side then front (idle, swing, roll, knockdown, 12 s a round) and logs `[hb] vi <frame> f<game frame> ...` (the capsule and bones over the floor). Mupen's shot number is the game frame + ~3, not `vi`.
- **Clean README shots:** `HLE_SHOT_CLEAN` / `HLE_NO_HUD`.
- **Frame-exact video:** shot every frame, `run.sh rom "$(seq -s, 900 1 1100)"` (comma list, not a range), then
  `ffmpeg -framerate 30 -pattern_type glob -i '*.png' -vf scale=640:-2,format=yuv420p -c:v libx264 -crf 18 out.mp4`. Lower `-framerate` or `setpts=4*PTS` for slow motion.
- **Sheets / side-by-sides:** `-vf "scale=320:-1,tile=6x4" -frames:v 1`; `hstack`/`xstack` with `crop=...,scale=...:flags=neighbor`.
- **Audio:** the emulator runs `--audio dummy`, so sound is never captured from it. Build A/B clips from the asset files with ffmpeg `concat` (old, 1 s `anullsrc`, new); loudness via `astats` / `volumedetect` / `ebur128`. `tools/boss_sfx_map.py` makes a page of every Bonus move's sounds (LUFS, gain); `tools/boss_sfx_levels.py` needs numpy (Blender's Python has it). Music loops: `tools/music_loop.py` (beat-grid loop points written as a WAV `smpl` loop; needs `--bpm`, see its header), `HLE_LOG_MUSICPOS` logs `[musicpos]` every 2 s (the jump back shows as the position dropping).
- **LLE reference shots:** `~/n64/emu/ref/make_lle_refs.sh` (angrylion + cxd4 via `run_lle.sh`).

## Measure
- **FPS / CPU:** `HLE_LOG_FPS` (`[fps]` every 2 s, per-part ms, tri/vert counts; meant for Ares). `HLE_TEST_PERF` (rsp-hle CPU %, audio queue, worst frame). GPU: `HLE_PROF_SERIAL`, `_DRAGON` (also the Shackled Sun's parts: `[sunprof]`), `_BOSS`, `_WAITEND`.
- **Pacing:** `HLE_LOG_HITCH`, `HLE_LOG_JITTER`, `HLE_LOG_LATENCY`. `HLE_TEST_TASKS` checks one graphics task per frame (catches black-frame flicker shots can't).
- **Memory:** `HLE_TEST_BONUS` logs the lowest free heap; `HLE_LOG_FPS` logs `[arena] ... heap N KB free ..., least M` every 120 frames (sampled each frame). The Tower's low point on one path (Ares, 4 MB): `HLE_TEST_TOWER_RUN HLE_RUN_CLIMB HLE_RUN_STRIKE HLE_RUN_TOPUP HLE_RUN_FLOORS=5 HLE_TOWER_SB_LAST HLE_LOG_FPS` (a 20 s sword fight a floor, never dying, Shadowborne last of the Bonus floors: the order is otherwise random). Ares keeps `<rom>.eeprom` beside the ROM: a second climb on the first one's save drafts other boons (Shrine packs, pacts) and lands elsewhere (510 vs 517 KB); from a fresh dir the same ROM repeats to the KB. By caller: add `HLE_TEST_LEAK` (hardware: `TEST_LDFLAGS=` the same `--wrap` list as below, plus `--wrap rspq_block_begin_reuse`, which charges a block's buffers to whoever recorded it); its 256 KB table doesn't fit at 4 MB, so run it with the Expansion Pak. Each floor (and `HLE_TEST_BONUS`'s fight) then logs `[bycaller] <floor> <ra> <bytes> in <n>` (every block of 4 KB and up as `big`, matched to `[leak] asset_load` names by pointer) and `[low] tag <floor>`: the least free heap, caught at the allocation that made it.
- **Move starts on Mupen's frame numbers:** `HLE_LOG_MOVE_MS` (Shadowborne: `[movems] <state> <VI frame>`, counted from boot; shot `N` is VI frame `N`): line up before/after videos of builds that drift apart.
- **Choices:** `HLE_LOG_CHOOSE` (Shadowborne: the distance, angle and candidate moves at each pick, then the pick).
- **Boss hit:** `HLE_FK_STAGGERME` (the Fallen Warrior staggered every 8 s, `[fk] staggered`), `HLE_DRAGON_HITME` (Shadowborne takes a sword hit every 1.5 s, a finisher's every 6 s: the flinch; `[flinch]` with `HLE_LOG_MOVES`). The passive dummy and the bots never damage the boss.
- **Fallen Warrior's transitions:** `HLE_LOG_POP` (`[pop] st <from>><to> clip <from>><to> peak <accel> pt <point> yaw <turn accel> body <accel> at <ms> late <body> <sword>` for 0.4 s after each clip or move change; `[popclip]` each clip's own, every 30 s; model-space glTF units/s^2, points hands/head/hips/feet/sword tip). The knight logs the same with it (`[ppop] st <from>><to> clip <from>><to> lock <main clip> peak <accel> pt <point> at <ms> late <accel>`, points hands/head/hips/feet; `[ppopclip]` the worst outside changes every 30 s). `HLE_POP_REF` turns the crossfades off: the clips' own motion to compare against (use the `late` values, past the hard cut). Ranking: `tools/fw_poprank.py <blended logs,> <ref logs,>`. `HLE_FADE_FROZEN`: the fade's start pose held still, as it was (no carried motion), to A/B the carry. `HLE_POP_TRACE=<clip>` prints every frame of the changes into that clip (`[poptr]`: each point's accel, the fade, and the blade/grip/seat/chain layers' weights).
- **Fallen Warrior's footwork:** `HLE_LOG_FWPATH` (`[fwpath] <bearing round the player> <distance> st <state> clip <n>`: how much it circles, and which clip it walks on). `HLE_LOG_FWCLIP`: the closest its horns come to its pauldron (< ~0.05 glTF units is touching) and to its blade's line (the blade is ~0.13 to each side of it, the guard ~0.76), posed, every 2 s; with `HLE_LOG_FWPOSE` it also logs the blade and the upper arm in the head's space every 4th frame (`[fwpose]`), and `tools/fw_clip_score.py <log> [PATH R0]` scores the horns (or a trial horn shape, without a rebuild) against them: the share of frames, by state, with the blade through a horn or the pauldron touching one. `HLE_FW_CLOSECAM` (`HLE_FW_CLOSECAM_D` 26, `_DROP` 2.5 below the head, `_SPIN` 0.0004 rad/ms, `_A` from its facing): the camera orbits close round its head and shoulders, or held (`_SPIN=0.0f`); README portrait: `_D=48.0f _DROP=7.0f _SPIN=0.0f _A=0.25f` with `HLE_SHOT_CLEAN` (which also hides the lock-on marker). `HLE_FK_NOLOOK`, `HLE_FK_NOHOLYKEYS`: its head as the clips have it.
- **Rig:** `HLE_LOG_RIG` (Shadowborne, per move: worst leg reach, neck and torso stretch as drawn, the head's nearest pass of its own body).
- **Out of the hall:** `HLE_TEST_OOB` (with `HLE_TEST_FREEZE_BOSS`): a full knockdown shove with the knight's back to each wall, the stairs' slanted ones too; `[oob]` logs where he ends up and `OUT OF THE HALL`. `HLE_OOB_SLOW_MS=<ms>` stalls each frame (40: Delta's slow frames, where the shove tunnelled through), `HLE_OOB_DIST` (68) how far from the wall's middle he starts.
- **Hits:** `HLE_LOG_MOVES` (`[move]` then `[hit]`), `HLE_LOG_REACT` (with `HLE_DUMMY_REAL`: `[react] blow <n> lost <n> flinch|knockdown|shove`, the reaction from the full blow), `HLE_LOG_HURTBOX`, `HLE_LOG_HITVOL`, `HLE_FK_HITLOG`, `HLE_TEST_ARC_REACH`, `HLE_LOG_JUMPS`. Sound timing: `HLE_LOG_SFX` with `HLE_LOG_MOVES` (`[sfx] ms index d <distance heard from>`).
- **Same pixels after a rewrite:** `HLE_TEST_T3D_EXACT`.
- **HLE block replay** (hle/replay.c): `HLE_TEST_REPLAY_CHECK` runs every replayable block normally and compares it with its recording (display list, scratch, matrix sources, state left): `[hle] replay: ... check N ok M BAD` with `HLE_TEST_PERF`, `[replay] MISMATCH` on any difference. `HLE_NO_REPLAY` interprets every block (the reference for pixel comparisons). `HLE_TEST_VCACHE_VERIFY` checksums the source of every vertex/strip cache hit (`[vcache] CHANGED` if data cached as static was rewritten). With `HLE_TEST_PERF`: replay counts, replay time by part, time inside recorded blocks.
- **Load/unload stress:** `HLE_TEST_CYCLE` loops title -> fight -> title forever, a different way each cycle (Fallen Warrior intro watched / skipped early / at the drop / mid-landing, Restart, Sun, Shadowborne, story, death, a kill and its outro); `[cyc] title ... free N KB (first, +/-)` each cycle (a leak: it keeps falling), `[cyc] hb` heartbeats (a freeze: they stop), `STUCK` lines. Add `HLE_TEST_LEAK` with `HLE_EXTRA_LDFLAGS="--wrap malloc --wrap free --wrap calloc --wrap realloc --wrap memalign --wrap malloc_uncached --wrap malloc_uncached_aligned --wrap asset_load --wrap rspq_block_end"`: at cycle 7 it lists allocations from cycles 2-5 still held, with the caller (`addr2line -i -f -e <elf>`); slows frames (its scans), so don't read frame times from it.
- **Memory used before it's set:** `HLE_TEST_DIRTY_HEAP` fills freed heap with garbage at frames 400 and 900; compare shots with a clean run (it found the Fallen Warrior's unset matrix).
- **Emulated CPU cost:** `HLE_TEST_MEMBENCH` (Mupen charges every instruction the same, cached or not). `HLE_LOG_SIDECAM` logs boss/player positions with `HLE_DUMMY_SIDECAM`.
- **Heap layout:** `HLE_HEAP_PAD=<KB>` holds that much from boot (Ares speed shifts with layout: try a few). A build's fight plays differently from another's (the AI's rolls follow the clock), so fps across builds in live fights isn't like for like; repeat runs of one ROM are identical.
- **Compare like with like:** live fights diverge between builds (AI, timing): compare pixels on frozen scenes (`HLE_TEST_FREEZE_BOSS`, best with `HLE_DUMMY_SIDECAM`) and fps over long live runs.
- **Logs only:** one huge shot frame (`run.sh rom 40000`) plus `TIMEOUT`; batch in the background, then `grep 'IS64: \[dummy\]'`.

## Gotchas
- Mupen numbers new shots after old ones: empty the out dir first.
- The Read tool may show a cached image for a reused path: copy to a new name.
- Leftover Ares instances write to the same log ("Booted" twice): kill them first.
- Passive-dummy fps flatters a fight (59.8 vs 57.3 in a real Shadowborne fight): confirm with a full fight.
- Mupen won't boot the hardware ROM; its LLE run crashes at boot.
